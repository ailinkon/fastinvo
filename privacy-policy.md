Privacy Policy for FastInvo

Last updated: 16-Aug-2026

FastInvo ("the App") is developed by Ashraful Islam. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Data Storage
By default, FastInvo stores all your data — including invoices, quotations, client information, and business details — locally on your own device. This data is never transmitted to us or any third party unless you choose to enable optional backup features described below.

2. Optional Account & Backup
FastInvo offers an optional sign-in feature (via Google Sign-in) solely to back up and sync your data across your own devices. If you choose not to sign in, the App remains fully functional using only local, on-device storage. If you do sign in, your invoice and business data is stored securely via Firebase (a Google service) under your own account, accessible only to you.

3. Data We Do Not Collect
We do not collect, sell, or share your personal or business data with third parties. We do not display ads. We do not track your activity for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Services
If you enable optional sign-in, FastInvo uses Google Firebase Authentication and Firebase Firestore to manage your account and optional cloud backup. These services are governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Deletion
You can delete your locally stored data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the App. If you have enabled cloud backup, you may request deletion of your cloud-stored data by contacting us at fastinvoicd@gmail.com

6. Children's Privacy
FastInvo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: fastinvoicd@gmail.com
